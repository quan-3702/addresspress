AddressPress – How to use

Print addresses on envelopes and on sheets of sticky labels, from your own address book.

1. Add your addresses

  1. Open AddressPress.
  2. Under 2 · My return address type your own name and address, one line under the other.
  3. Click + Add an address. Type the name, then the address with one line for the street and one for the city, state and ZIP or postcode.
  4. Click Save, or Save and add another to keep going.

Just want to try it? Click ☰ More → Add some sample addresses.

To change an address, click its name in the list. To remove it, click the ✕ next to it.

2. Choose who gets a letter

Every address has a tick box. Only ticked addresses are printed. Tick all and Untick all are above the list. The number in the heading shows how many are ticked.

3. Print envelopes

  1. Under 1 · What to print click Envelopes.
  2. Choose the Envelope size. No. 10 is the long US business envelope; A7 fits most greeting cards; DL, C5 and C6 are the common European sizes.
  3. Choose the Lettering and Letter size. Tick Print my return address on envelopes if you want it in the top left corner.
  4. Look at the preview. The arrows beside it step through the envelopes. The dotted stamp box is only a guide and is not printed.
  5. Click 🖨 Print. In the Windows print window choose the same envelope size as the paper size, and Scale 100%.

Every printer takes envelopes differently – face up or down, flap left or right. Print one envelope first, or try on a sheet of plain paper cut to size, before you print them all.

4. Print label sheets

  1. Click Label sheets.
  2. Choose the Label sheet that matches the size printed on your pack of labels.
  3. Click Print a test page and print it on plain paper. Hold it behind a label sheet against a window: the boxes should sit on the labels.
  4. Click 🖨 Print with the label sheet in the printer. Choose the same paper size (Letter or A4) and Scale 100% – not “Fit to page”.

Start at label no. lets you use a sheet that already has some labels missing: type 4 to leave the first three empty. Labels are counted left to right, row by row.

Labels show → Only my return address fills the whole sheet with your own address.

5. If the print is off-centre

Printers often shift the page by a millimetre or two. Under If the print is off-centre type how far to move everything: for example 2 in Move right moves the text 2 mm to the right, −1.5 in Move down moves it 1.5 mm up. Print the test page again to check.

6. Long addresses

A line that is too long for the label or envelope is split onto two lines automatically. If there is still not enough room, the letters are made a little smaller so that everything fits.

7. Keep or move your address book

☰ More → Save all addresses as a CSV file saves your list in a file that Excel can open. Keep it as a backup.

Import addresses from a CSV file… (Pro) adds addresses from a file – for example a list from Excel, Google Contacts or Outlook, or your own backup. Columns such as Name, First name, Last name, Street, City, State, ZIP and Country are recognised.

8. Free version and Pro

The free version prints envelopes and labels as often as you like and keeps up to 10 addresses. Pro is a one-time purchase and adds unlimited addresses, CSV import and your own envelope size. Click ★ Get Pro at the top to see the price, or Restore purchase if you bought it before.

9. If something goes wrong

  • The text is printed in the wrong place on the envelope: check the envelope size in the print window, turn the envelope the other way in the tray, or use the off-centre boxes.
  • The labels drift further off towards the bottom: the printer is scaling the page. Set Scale to 100% or Actual size.
  • My addresses are gone on another PC: they are stored on the PC where you typed them. Move them with a CSV file.
